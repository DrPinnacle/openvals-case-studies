# Contributing

Powered by Dr.Pinnacle

Keep study IDs stable. Add a self-contained folder with research question, evidence label, methodology, data provenance, tests, case-level results, limitations, and license notices. Update one row in the root index. Distinguish simulated results from empirical model or product evidence. Do not contribute private records, credentials, unauthorized targets, or harmful payloads. Review DPCL.rtf and artifact-specific notices before contributing.
